## Research

### AI in Cross-cultural Design Research
<span class="research-project">CrossProbe</span>  
HCII 2024 Best Paper Award

Scaling design research with AI usually means flattening the cultural texture you came to study. CrossProbe is a multi-agent platform that runs interviews in each participant's language and cultural frame, treating cultural adaptation as part of the method rather than a post-hoc correction. Comparative experiments showed it preserved interview-level depth while cutting preparation time by 80%.

### Multisensory Memory in Extended Reality
<span class="research-project">Olfactory Cues in MR</span> — In progress

Most XR training breaks at the moment the headset comes off: the sensory context of learning doesn't transfer to where the skill actually gets used. This study tests whether scent — the one modality that travels across both environments — can act as a memory anchor between them. Matched olfactory contexts produced significantly higher transfer accuracy and lower cognitive load than mismatched ones (n=48, 2×2 between-subjects).
