## Research

{{< work title="Olfactory Cues in MR" year="2026" tag="IEEE TVCG · ISMAR 2026" video="files/works/olfactory-mr.mp4" poster="images/works/olfactory-mr.jpg" role="Co-author — study design, data collection, methodology" >}}
Participants learn a procedural skill in a digital-twin mixed reality environment, then perform it in the real world. We test whether a matching scent in both settings works as a memory anchor that carries the skill across.
{{< /work >}}

{{< work title="CrossProbe" year="2023 – 2025" tag="HCII 2024 Best Paper Award<br>China Collegiate Computing Competition (4C), 2nd Prize" images="images/works/crossprobe-hero.jpg, images/works/crossprobe-ui.jpg" role="Lead — feature and page breakdown, interface design, testing" team="Tao Gong; advised by Yue Qiu and Nan Yang" link="https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-60913-8_27" linktext="Paper" >}}
A multi-agent AI platform for cross-cultural design research: AI respondents help refine the interview outline, survey bots run conversations in each participant's language, and the analysis is automated. It cut preparation time by 80% while keeping interview-level depth.
{{< /work >}}

## Design
